-- Driver 2: 1:12.050 (72.050s) -> If milliseconds aren't divided by 1000 or parsed properly, or if integer division is used (CAST AS INT / 1000 = 0), Driver 1 and Driver 2 tie at 72.0s!
-- Let's make Driver 2 have driverId=1 and Driver 1 have driverId=2 so that if milliseconds are truncated to 0 via integer division, Driver 2 (id=1) wins instead of Driver 1 (id=2)!
-- Wait: let's set Driver 1 (id=1, Gasly) to '1:12.500' (72.5s) and Driver 2 (id=2, Ocon) to '1:12.050' (72.05s).
-- If milliseconds are truncated (integer division / 1000 = 0), both have 72.0s, and id=1 (Gasly) wins by tie-breaker! With REAL division, id=2 (Ocon) wins (72.05 < 72.5)!
-- Also Driver 3 (id=3, Bottas) has '1:01:00.001' (has an hour component: 1 hour, 1 min, 0.001s). If hours are ignored, it looks like 1 min 0.001s (60.001s), beating everyone!

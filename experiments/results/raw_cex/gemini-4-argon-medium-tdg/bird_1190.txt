-- DATASET 2
-- Tests:
-- 1. SQLite Type Affinity / Lexicographical vs Numeric comparison on TEXT column "U-PRO".
-- In SQLite, "U-PRO" (TEXT affinity) compared to integer literals 0 and 30 converts 0 and 30 to '0' and '30'.
-- '2' > '0' AND '2' < '30' is TRUE lexicographically, whereas '5' < '30' is FALSE lexicographically (since '5' > '3'),
-- and '100' < '30' is TRUE lexicographically (since '1' < '3').

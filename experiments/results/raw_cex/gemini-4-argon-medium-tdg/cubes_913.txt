-- DATASET 3
-- Tests three-valued logic (NULL handling) in the anti-join predicate (name <> 'John Williams') and ORDER BY NULLS LAST.
-- In SQL, if employee.name is NULL, (name <> 'John Williams') evaluates to UNKNOWN, so the row is excluded from the subquery,
-- meaning NOT EXISTS evaluates to TRUE! A mutant rewriting NOT EXISTS to an inner join with (name = 'John Williams') will fail here.

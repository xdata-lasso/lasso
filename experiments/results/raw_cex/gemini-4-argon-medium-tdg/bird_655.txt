-- Tests wrong join key mutants (e.g., joining on T1.Id = T2.Id, T3.Id = T2.Id, or T2.LinkTypeId = T3.Id).

-- Here, postLinks.Id is 1 (matches T1.Id), PostId is 2, RelatedPostId is 3, LinkTypeId is 1.
-- Correct query returns nothing because PostId=2 does not have the target title.
-- Mutants joining on T1.Id = T2.Id or T1.Id = T2.LinkTypeId will falsely match this row.

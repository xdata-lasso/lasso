-- DATASET 2
-- NULL handling in Orders.user_id:
-- Crucial for differentiating `NOT IN` from `NOT EXISTS` and `LEFT JOIN ... IS NULL`.
-- When Orders contains a NULL user_id, `NOT user_id IN (SELECT user_id FROM Orders)` evaluates to UNKNOWN (empty result),
-- whereas `NOT EXISTS` or `LEFT JOIN` would still return 'Bob'.

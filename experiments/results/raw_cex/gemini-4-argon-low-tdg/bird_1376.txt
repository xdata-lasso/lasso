-- DATASET 2
-- Tests aggregate vs individual row evaluation (SUM(spent)/SUM(amount) or MAX(spent)/MAX(amount) with GROUP BY).
-- Event E2 has a single budget with ratio 0.85 (winner).
-- Event E1 has two budgets: one with 0.80, and one with 0.90 on a tiny amount, or vice-versa, to break aggregate mutants.
-- Specifically, E1 has B1 (spent 80, amount 100 -> 0.8) and B2 (spent 10, amount 10 -> 1.0? No, if E1 has 0.8 and 0.8, SUM is 0.8, MAX is 0.8).
-- To make SUM(spent)/SUM(amount) pick E1 while individual row picks E2:
-- E2: B3 (spent 90, amount 100 -> ratio 0.90) AND B4 (spent 0, amount 1000 -> ratio 0.0). Individual max for E2 is 0.90, but SUM ratio for E2 is 90/1100 = 0.081.
-- E1: B1 (spent 80, amount 100 -> ratio 0.80). Individual max for E1 is 0.80, SUM ratio for E1 is 0.80.
-- Gold query picks E2 (ratio 0.90). A mutant grouping by event and using SUM(spent)/SUM(amount) or AVG(spent/amount) will pick E1 (0.80 > 0.081 and 0.80 > 0.45).

-- DATASET 2
-- Tests multiple rows in 'submission' with NULLs, duplicates in non-PK columns, and a different row count in 'Acceptance' and 'workshop'.

-- mutants using COUNT(DISTINCT column), or mutants joining with 'Acceptance' / querying the wrong table.

-- DATASET 3
-- Tests the CROSS JOIN with the Orders table when Orders is empty, while Products and Order_Items are populated (via NULL order_id).

-- Mutants that optimize away the Orders table (since no columns from Orders are in the final SELECT) will fail and return rows.

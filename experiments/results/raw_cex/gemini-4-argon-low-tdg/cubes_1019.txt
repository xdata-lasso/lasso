-- DATASET 3
-- Mix of NULL and non-NULL 'enr' values, including 0 and negative/float edge cases.
-- Tests:
-- 1. Handling of NULLs in SUM vs COUNT.
-- 2. Filtering out NULL rows erroneously (e.g. WHERE enr IS NOT NULL when all are NULL, or affecting row count if DISTINCT is missing).
-- 3. Arithmetic precision with REAL numbers.

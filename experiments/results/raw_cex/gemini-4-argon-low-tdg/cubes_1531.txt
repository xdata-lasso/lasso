-- DATASET 2
-- Multiple students advised by the same instructor, plus one student with a NULL advisor, plus unreferenced students and instructors.
-- Tests:
-- 1. Missing DISTINCT (returns multiple rows of 3 instead of a single row of 3)
-- 2. Counting i_ID or DISTINCT i_ID instead of s_ID / * (i_ID count is 2, distinct i_ID is 1, while total rows is 3)
-- 3. INNER JOIN with instructor or WHERE i_ID IS NOT NULL (excludes the NULL i_ID row, yielding 2 instead of 3)
-- 4. GROUP BY i_ID or PARTITION BY i_ID (yields counts of 2 and 1 instead of 3)
-- 5. Counting from student or instructor table directly (student has 4, instructor has 2, advisor has 3)

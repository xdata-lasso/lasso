-- 'b', 'a', and NULL all have COUNT(DISTINCT molecule_id) = 1.
-- Without NULLS LAST in SQLite, NULL sorts first in ASC order.
-- With T2.element ASC NULLS LAST, 'a' must win over 'b' and NULL.
